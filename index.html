PHASE 1 — PRE-RNF

Before Your RNF — Candidate Journey

Complete all steps below before your RNF is issued. This covers exam preparation, system setup, FSC application, certificate upload, and the mandatory FTS Programme.

LEADER Action by the Leader CANDIDATE Action by the Candidate BOTH Both parties involved
A
➕ Add Candidate in iRecruit+ LEADER ▾

The leader logs into iRecruit+ and adds the new candidate to start the recruitment process. This triggers a PDPA consent email to the candidate.

  • 1Open iRecruit+ and tap "Add a New Candidate" from the home dashboard.
  • 2Select the recruitment type: Direct, Assign to, or Contract under.
  • 3Fill in the candidate's personal information: Full name, Mobile number, and Email address.

iLearn is accessed through the iRecruit+ Candidate Portal (not a separate login) until the candidate is contracted. All CMFAS study resources are here.

🏆 Achiever Track

🧗 Steady Climber Track
Complete exams over 8 weeks. Requires 1–2 hrs daily. Suited for working professionals balancing other commitments.

💼 EPS / FAS Application OPTIONAL BOTH ▾

The Entrepreneur Power Scheme (EPS) or Financial Advisor Scheme (FAS) is an optional financial incentive scheme available after the FSC application is submitted. The leader sends the invite; the candidate decides whether to apply.

ℹ️ When to apply: EPS/FAS application can be initiated after the FSC application is submitted. The interview can be conducted concurrently with the FSC/FC interview.

F
🎓 Foundation To Success (FTS) Programme MANDATORY CANDIDATE ▾

The FTS Programme is mandatory for all new FSCs and must be completed after passing all 4 CMFAS papers and before your RNF is issued. You cannot activate iPoS or conduct any sales without completing FTS.

  • 1FTS is held at least twice a month. Check with your leader for the next available dates and register early — slots fill quickly.
  • 2Attend 2 full days of classroom training at AIA. Ensure you attend both days in full — partial attendance does not count.
  • 3Complete the FTS eModules on the iLearn platform (accessible via your Candidate account before FSC code, or Agent account after).
  • 4Upon completion, your iPoS access will be unlocked — proceed to Post-RNF: iPoS Activation.
⚠️ Do NOT attempt to activate iPoS or engage any client until the FTS Programme is fully completed and your RNF notification has been received.
ℹ️ FTS eModules can be started on iLearn while awaiting your classroom training date. Check the iLearn dashboard under "My Training Journeys".

PHASE 2 — POST-RNF

After Your RNF — Systems Setup & Office Access

Once your RNF is issued, complete these steps in order. Each step has a dependency — do not skip ahead.

1
🪪 Receive Your FSC Code MUST DO FIRST Day 1–3 of RNF ▾

Your unique 5-digit agent code from Tied Distribution Operations Department. This arrives after RNF is issued.

  • aWait for your FSC code to be issued — it is your unique identifier within AIA.
  • bDo NOT engage in any sales activity until you have received your FSC code, completed FTS, and received your RNF notification.
⚠️ Important: You must have completed the Foundation To Success (FTS) Programme and received your RNF notification before conducting any sales.
2
📧 Activate Your AIA Branded Email Within 3 working days of Step 1 ▾

Your official AIA email (FirstNameLastName@aia.com.sg) is auto-created within 3 working days of your FSC code being issued.

  • 1Check your personal email for the AIA Welcome email from IT.
  • 2Check your mobile phone for an SMS containing your temporary password.
  • 3Sign in at login.microsoftonline.com using your AIA branded email and the SMS password.
  • 4Set up your authentication method — either register your phone number or download Microsoft Authenticator (recommended).
  • 5Accept the Terms & Conditions.
  • 6(Optional) Change your preferred display name or email within 7 days — this can only be done once.
ℹ️ Email format: FirstNameLastName@aia.com.sg (e.g. johnsmith@aia.com.sg). You have 7 days from creation to change it — one time only.
⚠️ Must set up Microsoft Authenticator first before proceeding to Intune setup.
3
📱 Download Apps via Microsoft Company Portal After Step 2 · ~30 mins ▾

Install core Microsoft O365 productivity apps onto your device via the Company Portal.

  • 1Search for "Intune Company Portal" on your App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android).
  • 2Install and open the app, then tap Sign In.
  • 3Enter your AIA branded email and password, then approve the sign-in via Microsoft Authenticator.
  • 4Tap Begin on the Company Access Setup page.
  • 5Tap Allow to download the Management Profile, then go to Settings → General → Profile to install it.
  • 6Tap Trust to enrol the device. Set a 6-digit device passcode when prompted (expires every 180 days).
  • 7Return to Company Portal — all checkmarks should turn green. Tap Done.
  • 8Under Featured Apps, install: Microsoft Outlook, Teams, OneDrive, Word, Excel.
⚠️ Set up Microsoft Authenticator before enrolling in Intune — otherwise your work account won't appear during sign-in.
ℹ️ Tip: Have a pen and paper ready to note down your new 6-digit device passcode. It must not have ascending or descending digits.
4
🌐 Login to AgIA (Agent Internet Access) After Step 2 ▾

AgIA is AIA's secure agent portal. It must be accessed using Microsoft Edge only.

  • 1Open Microsoft Edge browser (AgIA does not support other browsers).
  • 2Navigate to the Agent Internet Access (AgIA) page.
  • 3Click "LOGIN AGIA VIA BRANDED EMAIL".
  • 4Authenticate using your AIA branded email and approve via Microsoft Authenticator.
⚠️ Microsoft Edge only. AgIA Single Sign-On (SSO) will not function correctly in Chrome, Safari or Firefox.
5
📊 Download iPoS from AgIA After Step 4 ▾

iPoS (Interactive Mobile Office) is the core platform for all insurance transactions. Download it from within AgIA.

  • 1Log in to AgIA (using Microsoft Edge).
  • 2Navigate to the iMO Suite / iPoS section and download the app to your iPad.
  • 3After download, go to iPad Settings → General → Device Management.
  • 4Find the AIA enterprise certificate and tap Trust to authorise the app.
ℹ️ iPoS is primarily designed for iPad. You will use it for all policy quotations, submissions, and client transactions.
6
🔓 Activate iPoS Access FTS REQUIRED FIRST After completing FTS Programme ▾

You can only activate iPoS after completing your Foundation To Success Programme. This unlocks full transaction capabilities.

  • 1Launch the iPoS app on your iPad.
  • 2Tap ADD USER.
  • 3Tap Get AgIA Password to request a temporary password from AgIA.
  • 4Enter your FSC Code and the temporary password to complete activation.
⚠️ Do NOT attempt to activate iPoS before completing the FTS Programme. Access will be blocked until Foundation requirements are fulfilled.

🤝 Joint Field Visit (JFV) First 3 closed sales ▾

A Joint Field Visit (JFV) is required for your first 3 closed sales. Your leader observes and co-signs the visit. There are specific rules on applicant ownership and timing.

  • 1A JFV form must be completed for each of your first 3 closed sales.
  • 2Applicant owners and FHRs (Financial Health Reviews) must be kept separate — do not mix ownership between the two.

  • 4Fill in the form, get your leader to co-sign, and retain for records.
⚠️ Ensure each JFV is correctly documented with no timing overlaps. Incorrect or overlapping records may invalidate the submission.

NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES

Things to Know & Remember

Key policies every FSC must follow from Day 1.

🚫
No Cash Culture
AIA operates a strict no-cash policy. All transactions must be processed through official digital channels only.
⏱️
7-Day Change Window
You have 7 days to change your AIA branded email display name or preferred name — once only. After that, it's fixed.
📄
Update Particulars Within 7 Days
Any change to your personal particulars (address, contact, etc.) must be updated via the Change of Particulars Form within 7 days.
🪪
Access Card
Issued by Real Estate Dept via your agency location admin. Replacement card costs SGD 20. Keep it safe.
🔒
AgIA = Microsoft Edge Only
The AgIA portal and SSO function only on Microsoft Edge. Do not attempt to access via other browsers.
🛑
No Sales Before Compliance
Do not engage any client in a sales context until you have your FSC code, completed FTS, and received your RNF notification.
